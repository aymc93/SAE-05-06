An invitation to a culinary journey for toddlers

An invitation to a culinary journey for the little ones, with flavors from the halal world adapted to the smallest. Each bite, rich in essential nutrients, offers a unique experience of awakening the senses and tasting discovery, while respecting the richness of African culinary traditions and the specific needs of infant feeding.

Fish thieb

Exclusive recipe of Cheboudienne with Fish for baby, inspired by the famous Senegalese dish. Based on fonio, carefully selected vegetables, and delicately crumbled fish. Soft and melting texture adapted to the youngest.

Key nutritional values:

  • Calcium: for strong bones and teeth
  • Iron and copper: for optimal cognitive development
  • Magnesium and zinc: to strengthen the immune system
  • Vitamins B1, B2, B3: energy and proper functioning of the nervous system

Beef Mafé

An adapted version of the great classic from West Africa. Melting beef, stewed vegetables and sweet peanut sauce. Flavor and creaminess for optimal growth.

Key nutritional values:

  • Calcium: strengthens bones and teeth
  • Magnesium: muscle and nerve relaxation
  • Zinc and iron: immunity and brain development
  • Vitamins B1, B2, B3: energy and cognitive functions
  • Vitamin A: vision, skin and cell growth

Chicken Yassa

Inspired by the Senegalese dish, Yassa with Chicken is prepared with tender chicken breast, simmered vegetables and sweet onion sauce. Suitable for the palates of the youngest.

Key nutritional values:

  • Calcium: strong bones and harmonious growth
  • Magnesium and zinc: immunity and energy
  • Iron: cognitive development and vitality
  • Vitamins B1, B2, B3: nervous system
  • Vitamin A: vision, skin and cell regeneration
