My story

How Madoudinette was born : a need, an idea, a mission

At the origins

After a trip to Senegal, I was confronted with a simple but important observation: there was no offer of small pots that combined flavours of the world, respect for halal beliefs and nutritional quality. This lack caught my attention: babies today have little access to a real diversity of flavors adapted to their needs.

The birth of Madoudinette

From this observation, the idea of Madoudinette was born: to offer small halal pots, organic and inspired by flavors of the world adapted to toddlers. My goal: to highlight the discovery of new flavors through food diversification, while respecting the religious beliefs of families.

Today

Madoudinette is a small company that works with local and international producers to obtain certain exotic products, as well as with an infant dietitian in order to develop adapted and balanced recipes. It also favors products from local sectors and cooperatives. The objective is to offer a varied range of sweet and savory small jars, inspired by flavors from around the world, with as first step recipes from West Africa already made, such as mafé, yassa or thieboudiene.

Our commitments

Beyond the taste, here is how we concretize our commitment: health, ingredients, environment, safety and cultural transmission.

🔬 Health & nutrition

Recipes adapted to the needs of infants, to support their growth and cognitive development. Rich in calcium, iron, zinc and vitamins, without added sugars, preservatives or artificial flavors.

🌿 Ingredients & quality

Simple and recognizable raw materials, organic as much as possible, with African cereals like fonio, seasonal vegetables and halal proteins. Short and transparent recipes, regular quality checks.

🛡️ Security & traceability

Strict standards, irreproachable hygiene, recipes validated by an infant dietitian, traceability and clear information for parents (allergens, composition).

🤝 Community & Equity

Support for local producers and responsible practices, transparent partnerships and fair trade, to make quality food accessible to the greatest number.
